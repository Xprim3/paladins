# Blood Paladins - Branding Guide

## Family Information
- **Family Name**: Blood Paladins
- **Main Alliance**: Blood Paladins
- **Academy**: Paladins Academy
- **Farms**: Paladins Farm1, Paladins Farm2, Paladins Farm3

## Color Palette - Dark Theme

### Primary Colors
- **Blood Red**: `#DC143C` (Crimson Red) - Primary brand color, CTAs, highlights
- **Dark Blood Red**: `#8B0000` (Deep Red) - Darker variant for gradients
- **Paladin Gold**: `#FFD700` (Gold) - Secondary accent, represents nobility
- **Dark Gold**: `#B8860B` (Dark Goldenrod) - Darker gold variant

### Background Colors
- **Dark Background**: `#0A0A0A` (Near Black) - Main background
- **Dark Surface**: `#1A1A1A` (Dark Gray) - Section backgrounds, cards
- **Dark Border**: `#2A2A2A` (Medium Gray) - Borders, dividers

### Text Colors
- **Light Text**: `#FFFFFF` (White) - Primary text on dark
- **Muted Text**: `#B0B0B0` (Light Gray) - Secondary text
- **Gold Text**: `#FFD700` (Gold) - Accent text, highlights

### Usage
- **Blood Red**: Headings, CTAs, important elements, hover states
- **Paladin Gold**: Accents, highlights, special information, links
- **Dark Backgrounds**: Main page background, section backgrounds
- **Light Text**: All text on dark backgrounds
- **Dark Surfaces**: Cards, elevated elements, borders

## Typography

### Headings
- **Font**: Bold, strong serif or sans-serif
- **Style**: Confident, authoritative
- **Examples**: "Cinzel", "Playfair Display", "Bebas Neue", or "Montserrat Bold"

### Body Text
- **Font**: Clean, readable sans-serif
- **Style**: Professional, clear
- **Examples**: "Inter", "Roboto", "Open Sans"

## Visual Style - Dark Theme
- **Dark & Dramatic**: Deep black backgrounds with blood red and gold accents
- **Professional & Powerful**: Strong, confident aesthetic with dark theme
- **Medieval/Fantasy**: Subtle touches that evoke paladin nobility
- **Game-Themed**: Appropriate for Rise of Kingdoms alliance
- **Welcoming yet Serious**: Dark theme creates serious, elite atmosphere
- **High Contrast**: Light text on dark for excellent readability
- **Elegant**: Gold accents add luxury and nobility feel

## Logo Concept
- Shield with cross (paladin symbol)
- Blood drop or sword element
- Simple, recognizable mark
- Works in single color (red or gold)

## Brand Personality
- **Strong**: Powerful and capable
- **Noble**: Honorable and trustworthy
- **Professional**: Serious about the game
- **Welcoming**: Open to new members
- **Organized**: Well-structured family/alliance system
